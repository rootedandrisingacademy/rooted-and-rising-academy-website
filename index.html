About the Academy

Built for adults who are ready to move forward.

Rooted & Rising Academy exists to help adult learners continue their education in an environment that values growth, responsibility, practical readiness, and the realities of adult life.

Our public information is intentionally clear about what is available today. Program details are published only after the Academy completes its internal review and release process.

What to expect

Clear steps. Practical preparation. Human support.

We are building an Academy experience that respects where learners are starting and keeps the next step visible.

01

Flexible progress

An adult-centered approach designed to make continued education more reachable alongside work, family, and other responsibilities.

02

Practical readiness

Learning is connected to communication, everyday problem-solving, life preparation, and readiness for the world beyond the classroom.

03

Written next steps

Enrollment status, required actions, and important decisions are communicated clearly rather than left to guesswork.
